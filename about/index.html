I'm a Staff Web Engineer at Anghami and OSN+, where I build the web and smart-TV apps for both streaming services. I specialise in video playback and DRM: getting protected content to start fast and play smoothly on every browser and TV it ships to. It's my favourite kind of work: when it's done right, nobody notices, they just watch.

Skills: TypeScript, JavaScript, React, Preact, Node.js, C++, Go, Rust, Lua, Docker, GitHub Actions, Linux and OpenWrt.

Beyond the web I like it close to the metal: low-level programming, emulation, C++ and graphics, for the simple joy of watching something boot. I also maintain openingh.nvim and neogruvbox.nvim for Neovim and GoWAN for OpenWrt, and I've had patches merged in Preact, LunarVim and nvim-tree. The home page of this site is an 8086 PC emulator I wrote, booting operating systems, games and demos I also wrote, which is what happens when a web engineer is left alone with an assembler.
